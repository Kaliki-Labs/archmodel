# CLAUDE.md

- `CONTEXT.md` is the glossary. Use its terms in code, docs and output.
- `docs/adr/` records decisions that are hard to reverse.
- Validate against the rendered examples in kaliki-template (`examples/rendered/*`), not only synthetic fixtures.
